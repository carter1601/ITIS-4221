CSRF Demonstration: forged friend approval

TuneStore uses a GET request: GET /Tunestore2020/addfriend.do?friend=attacker1 to approve a friend request. Because the victim's browser automatically attaches a session cookie to that request, sending the request in the victim's broswer will approve the friend request without their intentional approval.
